# Open questions

## Product alignment

- Who is the first buyer and who are the first daily users?
- Is the first segment primary, secondary, or staged across both?
- What city or province is the first operating scope?
- What single risk should the prototype validate first: identity, representation, lifecycle, or data visibility?

## Governance and permissions

- Who can create, verify, approve, correct, merge, and retire records?
- Is a seller account required, or is consent represented through documents and an authorized party?
- Which regulator roles define standards, own source data, and supervise activity?
- Which fields are public, member-only, organization-restricted, regulator-restricted, or consent-based?
- Which Organization or data owner approves each Access Request type?
- Which Restricted Field groups require dual approval for Break-glass Access?
- What retention and notification policy applies to sensitive-read audit events?
- Which platform-support actions can System Admin perform from metadata without opening business content?

## Property and Listing

- What is the canonical identity strategy for landed property, project units, and multi-parcel assets?
- Can sale and lease Listings coexist for one Property?
- What constitutes a duplicate active representation, and which exceptions apply?
- What is the approved Vietnam lifecycle and its transition guards?

## Data and integration

- What source is authoritative per field and jurisdiction?
- How are conflicting source records reviewed and corrected?
- Which integrations are available for cadastral, planning, tax, identity, license, and consent data?
- What retention, replay, reconciliation, and dispute policies apply?
