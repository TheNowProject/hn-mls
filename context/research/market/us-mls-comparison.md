# United States MLS capability comparison

## Structural model

`SOURCE CLAIM`: U.S. primary sources describe an MLS as governed cooperation, data, entitlement, licensing, and distribution infrastructure. Matrix, Paragon, Flexmls, and similar products are front-end systems used to access that infrastructure; IDX, VOW, syndication, showing, lockbox, CMA, tax, CRM, and transaction tools are downstream capabilities.

`INFERENCE`: The recurring mature capability groups are participant governance, listing lifecycle and data quality, property history, field-level distribution, cooperation, compliance, and a broad integration ecosystem. Structural weaknesses include fragmentation, regional variation, data licensing complexity, vendor dependence, inconsistent downstream freshness, and policy change costs.

## Relevance to VMLS

The following remain `PROPOSAL`:

- Preserve Property identity separately from each Listing.
- Represent membership, office/organization scope, entitlements, and purpose explicitly.
- Treat distribution as governed projections rather than unrestricted record copies.
- Use standards and idempotent integration contracts where partner systems exist.
- Keep CMA comparable selection human-reviewed.
- Separate marketplace experience from the legal, identity, policy, and audit planes.

U.S. rules, compensation practices, association membership, deadlines, and licensing policy are not Vietnam requirements. Translate Member/Office concepts through Party, Organization, Membership, Role, and Entitlement rather than copying them literally.
